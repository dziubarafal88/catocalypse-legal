1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Stackbit – Natalia Dziuba ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the mobile game CATOCALYPSE (the "Game") on Android or iOS. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

The Game does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any login credentials.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Game, the following technical information is collected by us and/or our service providers:

  • Advertising identifier — Android Advertising ID (AAID) or iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), used to serve ads. On iOS, this requires your explicit consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

  • Gameplay data — best score, coin balance, ad/IAP events. Stored locally on your device unless you sign in to Google Play Games / Apple Game Center for leaderboards.

2.3 In-app purchase data

If you purchase "Remove Ads", payment processing is handled exclusively by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed, used to grant the in-game entitlement.

  • To submit your score to Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center if you opt in to leaderboards.

  • To comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting, fraud prevention).

3a. Legal basis for processing (GDPR Article 6)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for personalized advertising and any optional analytics, collected via the in-app GDPR consent form. You may withdraw consent at any time from the Settings menu; withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of past processing.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — for processing your in-app purchase (Remove Ads) and granting the corresponding entitlement.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — for non-personalized contextual advertising, crash diagnostics, security, and fraud prevention. You may object to such processing at any time by writing to stackbit.n.dziuba@gmail.com.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — for tax and accounting records related to purchases, under Polish law (Ustawa o rachunkowości, Ordynacja podatkowa).

We do not use your data for automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Art. 22). Standard ad serving by Google AdMob is the only automated processing, and you may opt out via the consent form or device-level ad personalization controls.
