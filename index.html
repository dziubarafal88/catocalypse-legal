1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Stackbit – Natalia Dziuba ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the mobile game CATOCALYPSE (the "Game") on Android or iOS. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

The Game does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any login credentials.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Game, the following technical information is collected by us and/or our service providers:

  • Advertising identifier — Android Advertising ID (AAID) or iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), used to serve ads. On iOS, this requires your explicit consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Device information — model, operating system version, language, country, screen resolution, network type (Wi-Fi / cellular).
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address (country-level only), used for ad personalization and analytics.
  • Gameplay data — best score, coin balance, ad/IAP events. Stored locally on your device unless you sign in to Google Play Games / Apple Game Center for leaderboards.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — anonymous reports that help us fix bugs.

2.3 In-app purchase data

If you purchase "Remove Ads", payment processing is handled exclusively by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed, used to grant the in-game entitlement.

3. How we use information

  • To display interstitial and rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • To enable optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads).
  • To submit your score to Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center if you opt in to leaderboards.
  • To analyze crashes and improve gameplay.
  • To comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting, fraud prevention).

4. Third-party services

The Game integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing (Android purchases, leaderboards) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Game Center / StoreKit (iOS purchases, leaderboards) — apple.com/legal/privacy

5. Advertising and consent (GDPR / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game will display a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before initializing personalized advertising. You may choose to consent or refuse. If you refuse, you will still see non-personalized advertisements based only on contextual signals.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time from the in-game Settings menu.

6. Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, the Game requests permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (used to identify the IDFA for advertising). You may grant or deny this permission. Denying it does not prevent you from playing the Game; you will receive non-personalized advertisements.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is rated PEGI 12 / ESRB Everyone 10+ and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Data retention

To exercise these rights, contact us at stackbit.n.dziuba@gmail.com. Most data is processed by Google or Apple, not by us directly — for advertising identifiers, please use the controls in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of personalized ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
